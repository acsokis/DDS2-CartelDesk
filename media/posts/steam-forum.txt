Title: DDS2 CartelDesk — free Windows planner with a phone companion

I've made DDS2 CartelDesk, a free local companion for Drug Dealer Simulator 2. It reads your saved campaign and helps you find your inventory, plan recipe supplies, production equipment and transfers, and open known locations on the map.

The Windows app has Start / Stop controls and a local QR code for its portrait phone interface. Both use the game's FOW_Target.exr exploration mask, with a fog toggle and optional exploration sharing. Map markers, legend and empty-search results are visible. Filters and map movement have been optimized.

Planner lists support add, delete, quantities, save, favorites and reset. Owned inventory and acquisition sources are separate: a missing ingredient still shows its known purchase location. Unknown prices, stock and compatibility remain unknown. Red triangle pings support sets, multiple selection, timed expiry and manual route stops.

Extract the Windows ZIP and run CartelDesk.exe; keep runtime, web and licenses together. It reads game saves and does not automate gameplay. 11 interface languages are included. Route lines are straight-line planning aids.
